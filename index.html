Daily Insights

Preserving Your Sight

Vision health extends beyond our clinic walls. Small daily rituals can significantly protect your eyes from the stresses of modern digital life.

  • The 20-20-20 Rule

    Every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds.

  • Optimal Hydration

    Maintain natural tear production by staying hydrated throughout the day.

  • UV Protection

    Always wear balanced UV-filter lenses, even on overcast days.
